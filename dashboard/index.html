Decision notes

How this dashboard should be interpreted by a marketplace or category team.

  • Review volume is the engagement proxy. The current dataset does not include sales quantity, so review count is used as a directional customer-activity signal.
  • ASIN is the product key. Product metadata can be joined later to unlock category, brand, and price-band views.
  • Quality flags are deliberately simple. They create a transparent first-pass watchlist before adding sentiment or support-ticket classification.
  • The marts are BI-ready. The CSV outputs can be connected directly to Power BI, Tableau, DuckDB, or a lightweight web dashboard.
